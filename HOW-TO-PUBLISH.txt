TapImagine - how to put it online (about 15 minutes, on your laptop)

1. Get your AI key (free to start)
   - Go to aistudio.google.com and sign in with Google.
   - Click "Get API key" -> "Create API key". Copy it. Keep it secret.
   - Turn on billing in Google Cloud for real use (each image costs a little).

2. Put the code on GitHub
   - Make a free account at github.com.
   - Click "New repository", name it tapimagine, click Create.
   - Click "uploading an existing file" and drag in everything from this
     folder (index.html, package.json, and the api folder). Click Commit.

3. Put it online with Vercel
   - Make a free account at vercel.com (sign in with GitHub).
   - Click "Add New" -> "Project" -> pick tapimagine -> Import.
   - Open "Environment Variables". Name: GEMINI_API_KEY  Value: your key.
   - Click Deploy. You get a link like tapimagine.vercel.app.

4. Test it on your phone. Share the link.

To change prompts later: edit api/generate.js on GitHub. Vercel updates
the site by itself in about a minute.
